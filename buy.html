Taking a floor

Purchase isn't open yet

The floor is reserved in the directory, but the payment path is not live. It is being built deliberately slowly, because taking 50 USDC from someone is the one part of this that cannot be fixed with a redeploy.

Three failures found in review are shaping it: a payment claim that anyone can trigger cheaply can exhaust the chain lookups and block everyone from buying; one millionth of a dollar sent at the right moment can lock a floor as "reserved" forever; and a wallet that routinely approves transactions someone else composed has given away the privilege that actually matters, whether or not anyone holds its key.

How it will work

  1. You pick a vacant floor and connect a wallet. Nothing is held or escrowed.
  2. Your wallet signs one USDC transfer, straight to the building's treasury. The transaction is one you can read: a transfer, to a published address, for 50 USDC.
  3. The building watches the chain read-only and matches your payment.
  4. The floor binds to the wallet that signed, at the database level, so one payment can only ever buy one floor.
